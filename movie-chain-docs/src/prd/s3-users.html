3Users and Their Jobs

Five people use this system, and they sit in three different relationships to tenancy. Only three of them belong to a chain at all — a distinction that shapes the entire identity design in §4.2.

Read Grace's row carefully

Grace is not a user of Nyati Cinemas. She is a member of the public who buys tickets, most often with no account at all, and who will buy from a different chain the week she travels. She is a customer of the platform, transacting with chains.

This is the single most consequential correction in this document. Treating her as a tenant-scoped user produces a product that stops working the moment she leaves town — see §4.2.

The story that must never be written

There is no story of the form “as a chain admin, I want to see how my competitors are performing” — and no story that would require one tenant's user to read another tenant's data for any reason, including benchmarking, support or troubleshooting. Where a genuine cross-tenant need exists, such as platform-wide analytics, it belongs to Purity in the control plane and is served from an aggregated store, never by relaxing isolation in the application.
